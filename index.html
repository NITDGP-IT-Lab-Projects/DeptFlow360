🏛️ System Architecture: Event-Driven Workflow State Engine

Architecture Design

DAWMS models academic clearances as a deterministic finite-state automaton. Transitions between states require explicit role capability tokens, pass quota guards, and produce tamper-evident audit records in the departmental ledger.

[DRAFT] --(Student Submit)--> [UNDER_GUIDE_REVIEW]
  ├── [GUIDE_ENDORSE] --(Quota Guard OK)--> [UNDER_COORDINATOR_REVIEW]
  │    └── [COORDINATOR_VERIFY] --------> [UNDER_HOD_REVIEW]
  │         └── [HOD_APPROVE] ---------> [APPROVED / SANCTIONED]
  ├── [REQUEST_REVISION] -------------------------> [REVISION_REQUESTED] (Student Resubmits)
  └── [REJECT] -----------------------------------> [TERMINAL REJECTION]
